# Structural Uncertainty Limits Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how Hearthline handles incomplete, ambiguous, missing,
withdrawn, expired, or not-disclosed information without inferring sensitive
facts, generating person scores, or using proxy discrimination.

## Core rule

Unknown remains unknown.

The system must not transform missing information into:

- A probability.
- A confidence score.
- A compatibility estimate.
- A safety estimate.
- A risk estimate.
- A trust estimate.
- A desirability estimate.
- A response-likelihood estimate.
- A protected-identity estimate.
- A vulnerability estimate.
- A schedule or location estimate.

## Permitted responses to uncertainty

The system may:

- Exclude the field from a decision.
- Ask the member to confirm or edit a field before it becomes executable.
- Keep a requested capability unavailable until explicit consent is granted.
- Offer a remote, text-first, public-category, or self-directed alternative.
- Mark a source-labeled public attribute as unknown or outdated.
- Route a material policy ambiguity to human review.
- Fail closed for sensitive actions.

## Prohibited methods

Do not use:

- Bayesian person scoring.
- Latent-trait inference.
- Demographic imputation.
- Proxy classification.
- Behavioral prediction.
- Text embedding of private messages or narratives for matching.
- Image, voice, body, emotion, or biometric inference.
- Location or routine inference.
- Response-rate or engagement prediction.
- Confidence intervals attached to people or their private traits.

## Required member-facing language

> HLA does not guess missing information. You can add, edit, or leave this blank.
> Leaving it blank does not reduce your access to core features.
