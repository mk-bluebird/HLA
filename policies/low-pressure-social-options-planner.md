# Low-Pressure Social Options Planner Policy

## Purpose

The Low-Pressure Social Options Planner helps a member privately explore
optional, self-directed social possibilities using only current inputs the member
chooses to provide.

It is not a wellness-matching system, emotional-state assessment, treatment
tool, loneliness detector, crisis detector, recovery tool, vulnerability
classifier, or connection-outcome optimizer.

## Permitted inputs

The Planner may use only member-selected current inputs:

- Connection intent.
- Preferred format.
- Broad planning window.
- Pace and comfort controls.
- Cost preference.
- Broad setting preference.
- Notification preference.

## Prohibited inputs

The Planner must not collect, derive, retain, or use:

- Stress level.
- Emotional state.
- Loneliness.
- Isolation.
- Crisis.
- Trauma.
- Health.
- Disability.
- Recovery.
- Substance history.
- Financial need.
- Housing status.
- Employment status.
- Transport dependency.
- Calendar data.
- Exact schedule.
- Real-time availability.
- Location, routes, venue history, attendance, or device proximity.
- Reply rate, message volume, social graph, profile views, engagement, time spent,
  match count, date count, or relationship outcome.
- Protected identity or inferred identity.
- A prediction that a social option will improve wellbeing.

## Output requirements

Every output must be:

- Optional.
- Private by default.
- Dismissible.
- Editable where applicable.
- Non-repeating unless the member asks for recurrence.
- Free of urgency, guilt, scarcity, streaks, or social-performance framing.
- Available through text-first, keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion,
  low-bandwidth, no-camera, no-microphone, no-voice, and asynchronous paths.
- Accompanied by pause and stop-planning controls.

## Member-facing text

> These are optional social possibilities based only on the settings you chose
> for this planning session. HLA does not determine what you need or predict how
> an activity will affect your wellbeing.
