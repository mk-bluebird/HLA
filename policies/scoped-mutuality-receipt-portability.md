# Scoped Mutuality Receipt Portability Policy

## Purpose

Scoped Mutuality Receipts may prove a narrow, current, platform-defined state for
a stated purpose. They are not reputation, identity, trust, safety, or encounter
credentials.

## Permitted predicates

A receipt may prove only:

- Current 30+ adult-membership policy eligibility.
- Current reciprocal-interest-window state.
- Current direct-conversation consent state for a named scope.
- Current public-first meeting-plan mutual-acceptance state.
- Current named-public-venue disclosure-envelope state.
- Current withdrawal, expiry, or supersession state for a prior scoped receipt.

## Prohibited predicates

A receipt must never prove or imply:

- Reputation.
- Trustworthiness.
- Safety.
- Risk.
- Desirability.
- Popularity.
- Identity authenticity.
- Absence of reports, blocks, or restrictions.
- Number of matches, dates, encounters, or relationships.
- Sexual experience, sexual history, sexual availability, or consent quality.
- Health, sobriety, recovery, disability, finances, housing, employment, or
  legal status.
- Community standing.
- “Good member” status.
- Compliance personality.
- Future behavior.

## Receipt properties

Every receipt must be:

- Scope-bound.
- Purpose-bound.
- Expiring.
- Revocable.
- Non-transferable.
- Non-accumulative.
- Not public or searchable.
- Not visible in discovery.
- Not usable for matching, ranking, advertising, moderation, or default model
  training.
- Accompanied by plain-language limitations.

## External presentation

The initial implementation is intra-Hearthline only.

Any future external presentation requires:

- Member-initiated action.
- Recipient/service-specific consent.
- Minimal predicate disclosure.
- Short expiration.
- Active revocation check.
- No public DID, blockchain, wallet, or global identifier requirement.
- Privacy, security, accessibility, and policy review.
- A full ordinary non-cryptographic alternative.

## Required member-facing statement

> This receipt confirms only a limited current Hearthline state for the stated
> scope and time. It does not say that anyone is safe, trustworthy, consenting
> to anything else, or likely to behave in a particular way.
