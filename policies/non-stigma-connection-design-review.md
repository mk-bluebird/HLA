# Non-Stigma Connection Design Review Policy

## Purpose

The Non-Stigma Connection Design Review evaluates whether Hearthline features
allow adults to explore friendship, dating, romance, shared activities, casual
connection, and consensual intimacy without shame, coercion, surveillance,
identity profiling, or exploitation.

It evaluates product design and policy. It does not score people, groups,
communities, venues, identities, or types of relationship.

## Required review questions

Before release, reviewers must ask:

- Does the feature use neutral language for friendship, dating, romance, casual
  connection, intimacy, and undecided goals?
- Does it distinguish intent from consent?
- Does every escalation have a separate consent checkpoint?
- Can a member decline, pause, withdraw, block, report, or leave without penalty?
- Does it prevent money, housing, employment, debt relief, transportation
  dependency, drugs, gifts conditioned on intimacy, or basic needs from being
  linked to social or sexual access?
- Does it avoid treating casual connection as evidence of low worth, high risk,
  poor judgment, or lack of seriousness?
- Does it avoid treating relationship-seeking as evidence of higher worth or
  greater trustworthiness?
- Does it avoid requiring identity, sexuality, health, recovery, financial,
  location, or trauma disclosure?
- Does it avoid social-graph, engagement, presence, location, or behavioral
  surveillance?
- Does it provide text-first, keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion,
  low-bandwidth, no-camera, no-microphone, no-voice, and asynchronous paths?
- Does it provide clear reporting, human review, correction, and appeal?
- Does it state platform policy without giving legal advice or false safety
  guarantees?

## Prohibited metrics

Do not measure or generate:

- Social acceptance score.
- Casual-encounter acceptance score.
- Community permissiveness score.
- Sexual openness score.
- Intimacy likelihood score.
- Social influence score.
- Social graph centrality.
- Norm-conformity score.
- Community desirability score.
- Identity-group encounter rate.
- Person-level casualness score.
- Reputation, trust, safety, risk, or compatibility score.
- Member-level engagement, disclosure, encounter, or relationship outcome as a
  product success metric.

## Permitted release evidence

A review may examine:

- Policy language.
- Interface copy.
- Consent flow tests.
- Withdrawal and deletion tests.
- Block/report/appeal tests.
- Accessibility test results.
- Allowed-field and forbidden-field manifests.
- Privacy data-flow review.
- Non-commercial rule tests.
- Aggregated, thresholded, delayed product-clarity feedback.
- Voluntary, separately approved research findings where appropriate.

## Required outcome

The review outcome must be one of:

- Approved with documented safeguards.
- Requires remediation.
- Blocked from release.
- Requires policy or legal review.

The outcome applies to a feature version, not to any member or community.
