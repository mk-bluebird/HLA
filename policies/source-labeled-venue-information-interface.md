# Source-Labeled Venue Information Interface Policy

## Purpose

The Source-Labeled Venue Information Interface displays public information
published by venues, event organizers, transit agencies, government sources, or
other identified publishers.

It does not certify a venue as safe, accessible, appropriate, available,
private, trustworthy, suitable for a particular encounter, or capable of
creating consent between participants.

## Three distinct rule layers

### Venue or event policy

A venue or organizer may publish rules such as:

- Hours.
- Reservation policy.
- Entry policy.
- Recording policy.
- Conduct policy.
- Age policy.
- Capacity policy.
- Accessibility information.

Hearthline may display these only as source-labeled public information.

### Hearthline policy

Hearthline may enforce rules governing use of its own features, such as:

- Block and report controls.
- Consent checkpoints.
- No recording without separate permission.
- Non-commercial integrity.
- No forced disclosure.
- No stalking, harassment, or doxxing.

### Participant consent

Only participants may grant or withdraw consent for an interaction. Venue policy
and Hearthline policy never substitute for participant consent.

## Capacity and attendance boundary

Hearthline must not collect, display, estimate, infer, or use:

- Real-time venue capacity.
- Live crowd count.
- Attendance roster.
- Member check-ins.
- Counterpart attendance.
- Hearthline-member count at a venue.
- Arrival/departure state.
- Queue length derived from users.
- Nearby status.
- Venue occupancy prediction.

A published venue reservation policy or general capacity statement may be shown
only when clearly attributed to its official source and marked with retrieval and
review dates.

## Required display fields

Every venue information item must show:

- Venue or event title.
- Source publisher.
- Source URL.
- Source type.
- Retrieval timestamp.
- Review-by timestamp.
- Status.
- Plain-language summary.
- A statement that the information may change.
- A statement that Hearthline does not certify safety, accessibility,
  availability, suitability, or participant consent.

## Community-report boundary

Members may report:

- Broken or inaccurate source links.
- Outdated published venue information.
- Broken platform features associated with a meeting plan.
- Venue information presented without proper source attribution.

Members must not be asked to rate venue safety, trustworthiness, identity-group
suitability, encounter suitability, crowd quality, or other members' attendance.
