# Virtual Gathering Context Policy

## Purpose

A Virtual Gathering Context describes the rules, access options, consent
checkpoints, and closure conditions for an optional adult social interaction.

It does not certify that a venue, host, event, participant, or interaction is
safe, trusted, appropriate, inclusive, accessible, or likely to have a positive
outcome.

## Required member-facing statement

> This space describes its current rules and access options. Hearthline does not
> guarantee the behavior of participants, the availability of the space, or any
> particular outcome. You can leave, mute, block, or report at any time.

## Required controls

Every active context must provide:

- Leave without explanation.
- Mute and block.
- Report.
- A human-review path for submitted reports.
- An appeal path for material platform decisions.
- A text-first route.
- Keyboard and screen-reader access.
- A no-camera path.
- A no-microphone path.
- A no-voice path.
- A reduced-motion path.
- A visible statement about recording.
- A visible statement about data retention and expiry.

## Prohibited labels and ranking

The system must not create, display, or use:

- Trusted venue.
- Verified safe.
- Safe space certification.
- Trust score.
- Safety score.
- Risk score.
- Host reputation.
- Participant reputation.
- Venue popularity score.
- Community safety rating.
- “Best venue” ranking.
- Identity-group suitability rating.
- Encounter-success rating.

## Community-report boundary

Reports may concern:

- A rule violation within a Hearthline-managed interaction.
- Abuse of reporting or invitation features.
- Broken accessibility controls.
- Incorrect public-source information.
- Recording or media sharing without required permission.
- A platform-policy concern.

Reports must not become:

- A venue safety score.
- A public warning badge.
- A member reputation signal.
- A participant attendance record.
- A proxy for protected identity or community membership.
- A substitute for emergency services or legal advice.

## Noncommercial integrity

The Context must not facilitate social or sexual access in exchange for money,
housing, employment, debt relief, transportation dependency, drugs, gifts
conditioned on intimacy, or basic needs.

## Closure and retention

- A context must have an expiry or owner-controlled closure action.
- Closure must stop new entry and new invitations.
- The platform must not retain a roster of who attended for future discovery.
- Member exits, blocks, and reports must not become a public signal.
- Context-level audit data must be content-minimized and retention-limited.
