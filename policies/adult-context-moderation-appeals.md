# Adult-Context Moderation Appeals Policy

## Purpose

Adult-Context Moderation Appeals provide correction, human review, and appeal
paths for Hearthline moderation decisions affecting adult members, including
decisions involving consensual adult content, casual connection, media, sexual
discussion, reports, account restrictions, and feature access.

The process does not treat consensual adult content or casual connection as
inherently improper. It evaluates whether a specific action or content violated
a documented platform policy.

## Appealable decisions

A member may request correction or appeal for:

- Content removal.
- Media access restriction.
- Conversation or feature restriction.
- Account warning.
- Temporary suspension.
- Account closure.
- Discovery visibility restriction.
- Report outcome where an appeal is available.
- Denial of a member-owned feature capability.
- Decision based on incorrect account, policy, or evidence context.

## Required notice

Where safe and feasible, a material decision notice must include:

- The decision type.
- The effective date.
- The applicable policy category.
- A plain-language explanation.
- Whether the decision is temporary or continuing.
- How to request correction or appeal.
- Available accessibility accommodations for the appeal process.
- A statement that appeal use does not create a penalty.

The notice must not disclose:

- Reporter identity.
- Private report rationale.
- Another member's private messages, media, identity, location, or history.
- Internal anti-abuse methods that would enable evasion.
- A safety/risk/trust score or profile.

## Human review

Material restrictions require human review before final disposition unless a
temporary, safety-preserving measure is necessary. Temporary measures require:

- A documented reason code.
- A prompt review deadline.
- A path to submit relevant correction information.
- An appeal path once safe to provide one.

## Appeal process

```text
Member submits appeal
  → system validates scope and receipt
  → member receives confirmation
  → evidence is minimized and policy-versioned
  → qualified reviewer reviews
  → outcome is recorded
  → member receives outcome notice where safe
  → correction, escalation, or further appeal is available where policy provides
```

## Prohibited behavior

Hearthline must not:

- Require a member to disclose sexual history, diagnosis, health, recovery,
  identity, finances, housing, employment, or location to appeal.
- Treat appealing as evidence of risk, lack of cooperation, or bad faith.
- Use appeal history for discovery, ranking, advertising, or reputation.
- Create public appeal records or moderation leaderboards.
- Automatically deny appeals because content is adult, sexual, casual, queer,
  nontraditional, text-first, or accessibility-related.
- Make an automated model the final decision-maker for a material restriction.
