# Safety Support Action Menu Policy

## Purpose

The Safety Support Action Menu gives members direct control over actions they may
take when an interaction feels uncomfortable, pressured, unwanted, or unsafe.

It does not determine severity, diagnose danger, create a risk profile, or
automatically contact trusted contacts, authorities, emergency services, family,
employers, or other third parties.

## Member-initiated actions

A member may choose to:

- Leave an interaction.
- Mute an interaction.
- Restrict future interaction.
- Block another member.
- Report a platform-policy concern.
- Preserve selected content for a report where policy allows.
- Send a manually confirmed message to a selected trusted contact.
- Open device emergency options where supported.
- Read static, source-labeled emergency information.
- Request human review.
- Pause discovery.
- Withdraw a consent grant.
- Cancel a meeting plan.
- Remove a named-venue disclosure.
- Delete optional planning data.

## Prohibited system behavior

Hearthline must not:

- Infer an emergency from silence, timing, location, device state, messages,
  activity, non-response, or profile content.
- Assign a severity, safety, risk, trust, vulnerability, or credibility score.
- Automatically contact authorities, emergency services, trusted contacts,
  employers, family, providers, or other third parties.
- Share a member's location, route, identity, messages, meeting details, or
  private history without specific member action and authorization.
- Claim a message was seen, understood, or acted upon by a trusted contact.
- Claim an authority or emergency service was reached unless a device/platform
  integration directly and reliably confirms that limited event.
- Notify a counterpart that a member used safety controls or provide private
  reasons for a block, report, or restriction.
- Use safety-action use as a matching, ranking, advertising, moderation,
  reputation, risk, or trust input.

## Manual trusted-contact message

A trusted-contact message must:

- Require explicit confirmation immediately before send.
- Identify the selected contact.
- Show exact message content.
- Show the selected disclosure scope.
- State the delivery channel.
- Show an honest delivery result.
- Be cancellable before submission.
- Expire with the member's plan where appropriate.

## Reporting and human review

Reports are separate from blocking and are member-submitted. Material platform
actions require documented policy, appropriate human review, explanation,
correction, and appeal.

## Member-facing notice

> You choose what happens next. Hearthline does not automatically contact anyone
> for you. A submitted message does not confirm that a trusted contact saw it or
> can respond. You can leave, block, report, pause, or use device emergency
> options at any time.
