# Current Connection Intent Filter Policy

## Purpose

The Current Connection Intent Filter lets a member organize discovery options by
current, member-selected connection intent.

It is not a promise, a consent grant, a compatibility score, a sexual-availability
label, a relationship prediction, or a basis for social ranking.

## Supported current intents

- Friendship.
- Conversation.
- Shared activity.
- Dating.
- Romance.
- Intimacy.
- Casual connection.
- Relationship exploration.
- Undecided.

Members may select more than one intent, revise it, pause it, withdraw it, or
choose not to publish an intent.

## Mutuality requirements

A result may be shown only where:

- Both members independently selected active current intents.
- At least one current intent overlaps.
- Both members have active discovery consent.
- Current pace and boundary conditions do not conflict.
- No block, restriction, withdrawal, expiry, or policy boundary applies.
- Non-commercial integrity is satisfied.

## No unsolicited conversion

HLA must not:

- Treat a shared casual-connection intent as consent to sexual discussion,
  media, meetings, touch, intimacy, or sex.
- Treat dating or romance interest as consent to exclusivity.
- Treat friendship as consent to romance or intimacy.
- Prompt a member to “upgrade,” “convert,” or “escalate” another person.
- Penalize people for declining a different intent.
- Repeatedly surface an option after a member hides it.
- Use intent changes as a risk, trust, desirability, or engagement signal.
- Infer a person's sexual availability, relationship status, vulnerability, or
  emotional need from an intent selection.

## Required reminder

> This is a current connection preference, not a promise or consent. Either
> person can change direction, pause, decline, or leave at any time.

## Respectful response options

The interface must offer:

- “I’m looking for friendship only.”
- “I’m not discussing intimacy.”
- “I’d rather keep this as a shared activity.”
- “I’m not interested in changing the kind of connection.”
- “Thank you, but I do not think this is a fit.”
- “Please do not ask again.”
- Block and report.
