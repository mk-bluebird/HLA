# Access and Planning Preferences Policy

## Purpose

Access and Planning Preferences let a member select practical conditions for
communication, activities, and public-first meeting planning without requiring
disclosure of disability, diagnosis, health, mobility status, neurodivergence,
or personal explanation.

## Member control

A member may:

- Select practical preferences privately.
- Use no preference fields and retain full feature access.
- Edit, pause, withdraw, or delete preferences at any time.
- Choose whether to disclose a selected preference to a specific counterpart.
- Use a remote or text-first alternative.
- Choose independent arrival and departure.
- Browse source-labeled public venue or event attributes.

## Platform limits

Hearthline may show publicly published attributes from a source. It must not:

- Determine that a venue is accessible, inaccessible, safe, appropriate, open,
  affordable, or suitable for a member.
- Determine whether a member needs an accommodation.
- Require a diagnosis, document, medical proof, disability label, or explanation.
- Infer disability, health, competence, mobility, sensory profile, or identity.
- Match, rank, exclude, or filter people by access preference.
- Use access preferences for advertising, pricing, moderation profiling, risk,
  safety, trust, desirability, compatibility, or response prediction.
- Share a preference with a counterpart without explicit recipient-scoped consent.
- Treat an unknown venue attribute as evidence that a venue cannot work.

## Required venue information display

For any source-labeled attribute, show:

- Publisher/source.
- Source URL.
- Retrieval date.
- Review-by date.
- Status.
- Plain-language summary.
- Whether the attribute is unknown or not published.
- A statement that conditions can change and Hearthline does not certify the
  venue or event.

## Member-facing text

> You can choose practical preferences without explaining them. Venue and event
> details come from public sources and may change. Hearthline does not certify
> that an option is accessible, safe, open, or right for you.
