# Optional Minimal-Claim Credential Presentation Policy

## Purpose

Optional Minimal-Claim Credential Presentation lets a member present a narrow,
purpose-specific, time-limited claim without revealing unnecessary identity data.

It is not a general identity-verification system, public identity registry,
reputation system, trust certification, safety certification, or requirement for
ordinary participation.

## Permitted claims

A credential presentation may prove only:

- Current 30+ adult-membership policy eligibility.
- Current scoped consent grant.
- Current reciprocal-interest state.
- Current public-first meeting-plan mutual acceptance.
- Current named-public-venue disclosure state.

## Prohibited claims

A credential must not prove or imply:

- Legal identity.
- Real name.
- Exact age.
- Address.
- Government identifier.
- Document image.
- Face, voice, body, gait, or biometric trait.
- Identity authenticity.
- Sexual orientation, gender, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, health,
  recovery, finances, housing, employment, immigration, or legal status.
- Safety, trust, reputation, desirability, popularity, compatibility, risk, or
  relationship worth.
- Number of matches, dates, encounters, reports, blocks, or relationships.
- Absence of reports, blocks, or restrictions.
- Consent to anything beyond the exact current scope.

## Optionality requirements

- Every credential presentation path must have an equivalent non-cryptographic
  path where the feature can otherwise be provided.
- Credential presentation must never be required for ordinary discovery,
  conversation, friendship, dating, casual connection, community participation,
  reporting, blocking, accessibility controls, or quiet exit.
- Refusal or inability to present a credential must not reduce visibility,
  access, dignity, or social opportunity.
- Credential metadata must not be shown to other members.

## Privacy requirements

- Disclose the minimum predicate.
- Bind the presentation to the stated purpose and recipient where applicable.
- Use short expiration.
- Support revocation checks.
- Avoid persistent public subject identifiers.
- Do not require public DID publication, blockchain use, wallet creation, or
  public registry placement.
- Do not retain presentation data longer than needed for the stated purpose.
- Do not use credentials in advertising, discovery allocation, matching,
  moderation profiling, or default model training.

## Member-facing text

> This optional presentation confirms only a limited current Hearthline claim for
> the stated purpose. It does not reveal your identity details or say anything
> about safety, trust, reputation, or consent beyond the specific current scope.
