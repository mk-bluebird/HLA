# Optional Aftercare and Decompression Policy

## Purpose

Optional Aftercare and Decompression gives a member private, low-pressure tools
for reflection, rest, boundary review, notification quieting, and next-step
choice after any interaction or whenever the member chooses.

It is not a diagnosis, wellbeing assessment, encounter detector, safety rating,
relationship evaluation, reputation mechanism, or requirement to continue
contact.

## Entry conditions

The feature may open only when:

- The member explicitly opens it.
- The member uses a member-scheduled reminder.
- The member chooses it from an interaction menu.

The feature must not open automatically because of:

- A meeting plan.
- A call.
- Media sharing.
- Sexual-topic discussion.
- A date.
- A suspected casual encounter.
- A cancellation.
- A block.
- A report.
- Silence.
- Non-response.
- Device activity.
- Location.
- Calendar activity.
- Time of day.
- A member's message content.
- A platform inference about emotion, stress, trauma, recovery, health, or
  vulnerability.

## Permitted tools

- Quiet notifications for a member-selected duration.
- Pause discovery.
- Private reflection prompts.
- Private boundary and pace review.
- Private next-step note.
- Consent reminder.
- Quiet-exit controls.
- Block, mute, restrict, report, correction, and appeal controls.
- Optional source-labeled public information links selected manually by the
  member.
- Private trusted-contact message draft.
- Optional deletion or expiry review.

## Prohibited prompts

The feature must not ask:

- Was this person safe?
- Would you recommend this person?
- Did they satisfy you?
- Was the encounter successful?
- Did you have sex?
- Did you meet in person?
- Did they respect you?
- Did you use substances?
- Are you traumatized?
- Are you in crisis?
- Do you need help?
- Why did you not respond?
- Why did you leave?
- Will you see them again?
- Should HLA warn other members?

## Privacy and retention

- Reflection content is private by default.
- Local-first storage is preferred.
- The member may delete reflection content at any time.
- The platform must not use reflection content for matching, ranking, advertising,
  behavioral profiling, moderation profiling, research, or default model training.
- Any saved content must have a documented retention and deletion policy.
- No counterpart is notified that aftercare was opened, used, paused, or deleted.

## Member-facing text

> This space is private and optional. You can reflect, pause, change a boundary,
> or leave without explaining anything. HLA does not use this space to rate
> anyone or to decide what happened.
