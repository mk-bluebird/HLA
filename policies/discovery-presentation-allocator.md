# Discovery Presentation Allocator Policy

## Purpose

The Discovery Presentation Allocator organizes already eligible shared-activity or
connection options for a member's own browsing experience.

It does not rank people by compatibility, proximity, safety, risk, trust,
desirability, popularity, response likelihood, or social value.

## Eligibility before allocation

An option may be presented only after all required hard constraints pass:

- Current adult-platform eligibility.
- Current discovery consent.
- Current visibility state.
- Current connection-intent overlap.
- Shared activity or conversation context.
- Compatible selected pace and boundary conditions.
- Broad future activity-window overlap where relevant.
- Selected broad region or remote setting compatibility.
- No block, restriction, withdrawal, expiry, or policy prohibition.
- Non-commercial integrity condition.

## Permitted presentation modes

- Chronological.
- Activity category.
- Connection-intent category.
- Broad future time block.
- Public-first / remote-first category.
- Text-first / asynchronous category.
- Member-selected access or comfort controls where the member chose to use them
  for their own interface organization.
- One option at a time.
- Transparent rotation.
- Self-directed browsing.
- Show fewer options.
- Hide a category.
- Pause discovery.

## Prohibited ordering inputs

The Allocator must not use:

- Compatibility score.
- Proximity, distance, coordinates, GeoHash, map radius, location history,
  route, venue attendance, current location, or device proximity.
- Safety, risk, trust, reputation, desirability, popularity, or “quality” score.
- Report count, block count, complaint count, or absence of complaints.
- Contacts, social graph, uploaded address books, profile views, tags,
  workplaces, schools, or third-party relationships.
- Private messages, media, message sentiment, typing behavior, read receipts,
  response timing, online state, or account tenure.
- Health, disability, recovery, identity, sexuality, finances, housing,
  employment, legal history, vulnerability, or inferred personal state.
- Image quality, face/body/voice analysis, biometrics, or emotion inference.
- Advertising, payment, subscription, or engagement data.

## Rotation rules

Where more eligible options exist than display capacity:

- Use transparent rotation.
- Respect member-selected browsing categories.
- Prevent repeated presentation after hide or decline.
- Do not penalize a person for slower replies, fewer photos, fewer disclosures,
  private mode, paused discovery, or use of block/report controls.
- Do not reserve priority for high-engagement, highly viewed, recently active,
  or socially connected people.

## Required explanation

> This option is organized using the browsing category you selected. It is
> available because both people independently chose compatible current settings.
> Hearthline does not use distance, location history, venue attendance, safety
> scores, risk scores, popularity, private messages, profile views, or response
> likelihood.
