# Member-Requested Reflection Reminder Policy

## Purpose

A Member-Requested Reflection Reminder lets a member choose a private, optional
reminder about an intention they wrote or selected.

The reminder is not a social-health intervention, engagement tactic, streak,
performance metric, inactivity detector, or pressure mechanism.

## Defaults

- Default notification setting: off.
- Default recurrence: none.
- Default content: neutral and private.
- Default outcome after dismissal: no follow-up.

## Permitted reminder topics

- Review connection settings.
- Review discovery visibility.
- Review a saved draft.
- Consider a low-pressure activity option.
- Review a boundary or pace preference.
- Check whether a public-first plan still feels right.
- Pause, withdraw, or delete an optional connection object.
- Take a break from discovery.

## Prohibited reminder topics

- “You have not socialized enough.”
- “People are waiting for you.”
- “Do not lose your chance.”
- “Your match may disappear.”
- “You are falling behind.”
- “Keep your streak.”
- “You need to reply.”
- “You have been inactive.”
- “You seem lonely.”
- “You need support.”
- “You are at risk of missing connection.”
- “Improve your profile.”
- “Get more matches.”
- “Share more to find better people.”

## Notification rules

- No notification may be triggered by inactivity, reply delay, location, device
  activity, profile views, match count, calendar, behavior, or inferred state.
- A reminder may only run according to a schedule the member explicitly chose.
- A member may choose no notifications, in-app-only reminders, a digest, or a
  single reminder.
- Dismissing a reminder must not increase future reminder frequency.
- No reminder may contain another person's name, image, message, presence,
  location, or expectation.
- Reminders must remain available through an in-app text-first path.
- A member may pause or delete a reminder from the reminder itself.

## Required member-facing statement

> This is a reminder you chose. You can ignore, dismiss, pause, or delete it.
> Hearthline does not track whether you acted on it.
