# Public Resources Library Policy

## Purpose

The Public Resources Library provides source-labeled, publicly available
information that adults may independently browse.

It is not a need-assessment system, eligibility engine, referral service,
case-management tool, recommendation engine, emergency service, healthcare
provider, legal advisor, or benefit-application system.

## Member control

A person may:

- Browse without a Hearthline account where technically feasible.
- Select a resource category manually.
- Select a broad jurisdiction manually.
- Open an external official or publisher source independently.
- Leave the Library without creating a profile-level record of need.
- Use a screen-reader, keyboard, text-first, low-bandwidth, and reduced-motion path.

## Prohibited behavior

The Library must not:

- Infer that a member needs a resource.
- Recommend a resource based on profile, behavior, messages, identity,
  accessibility preference, relationship activity, or browsing pattern.
- Determine eligibility.
- Claim availability, capacity, affordability, safety, quality, licensure, or
  appropriateness.
- Refer a member automatically.
- Book appointments.
- Send a member's information to a provider.
- Share a member's contact, identity, location, meeting, health, recovery,
  finance, housing, employment, legal, or private history.
- Require disclosure before browsing.
- Require account login where a public path is feasible.
- Track clicks, reading time, search terms, category selection, or resource
  outcomes at the member level.
- Create a need score, assistance score, resource eligibility score, or
  vulnerability profile.
- Use Library interaction for discovery, matching, advertising, moderation,
  ranking, research, or model training.

## Required source fields

Every entry must contain:

- Resource identifier.
- Title.
- Category.
- Official or publisher source.
- Source URL.
- Source type.
- Jurisdiction.
- Retrieval timestamp.
- Review-by timestamp.
- Plain-language summary.
- Contact method as published.
- Accessibility information, if publicly available.
- Clear statement that eligibility, availability, suitability, and current
  capacity are unknown unless directly stated by the source.
- Status: listed, under_review, outdated, or removed.

## Required member-facing statement

> These links provide public information from listed sources. Hearthline does
> not know whether a resource is available, suitable, affordable, safe, or open
> to you. Opening a source does not share your Hearthline information with it.
